# ActiveRecord: Delete

There are two styles of row deletion: [destroy ](<ActiveRecord: Delete: Destroy.md>) and [delete](<ActiveRecord: Delete: Delete.md>).  The [destroy ](<ActiveRecord: Delete: Destroy.md>) style works on the instance. The [delete](<ActiveRecord: Delete: Delete.md>) style operates on the class.

The [delete](<ActiveRecord: Delete: Delete.md>) way bypasses some of Rails features and thus less preferred, use [destroy ](<ActiveRecord: Delete: Destroy.md>) instead.
