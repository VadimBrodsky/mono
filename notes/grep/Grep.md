## Useful options

- `r` recursive search
- `i` case insensitive
- `w` whole words
- `n` print the line numbers
- `l` print the file names only
- `c` counts the number of matches
- `B <num>` lines before the match
- `A <num>` lines after the match
- `C <num>` lines around the match
- `color` or `color=always` to turn on color highlighting of the matching word
- `exclude` to exclude a glob
- `exclude-dirs` to exclude whole directories
- `include` to include a glob
