The [`love.keypressed`](https://love2d.org/wiki/love.keypressed) function is a callback function that executed whenever we press a key, assuming we've implemented this in our `main.lua`, similar to [[./love.load]], [[./love.update]] and [[./love.draw]].

```lua
love.kepressed(key)
```
